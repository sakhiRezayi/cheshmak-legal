2. Who can use Cheshmak

Cheshmak is only for adults aged 18 or older. We ask for your birth date when you start, and our servers refuse any profile whose birth date shows an age under 18. If the birth date you enter shows you are under 18, the app stays closed on that device. If we learn that a user is under 18, we close the account. You can report a suspected minor from their profile (reason “May be under 18”).

6. Location

  • This app does not read your device location and does not ask for location permission.
  • Any location stored for your account by an earlier version of Cheshmak is not used by this app.
  • The Android network-state permission is added by the crash-reporting library; Cheshmak itself does not use it.
  • Photo location data (EXIF GPS) is removed on your phone and again on our server.

7. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted

Messages are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on our provider’s servers, which encrypt data at rest. They are not end-to-end encrypted: our systems can technically read them, and moderators read messages that are part of a report. Do not share passwords, financial details, or anything that could put you in danger if disclosed. Anyone you chat with can take screenshots.

Accounts restricted for breaking our rules. If your account was suspended or banned when it is deleted, we keep a minimal safety record for a fixed period of 2 years — to be confirmed, to protect other users and to defend legal claims (GDPR art. 6(1)(f), art. 17(3)(e)). It contains only:

  1. the restriction record (kind, reason category, dates, rule broken);
  2. the reports linked to it;
  3. the evidence kept for those reports (relevant messages, and copies or fingerprints of relevant photos);
  4. a keyed hash of your email address (a one-way code, not the address itself) so that the same email cannot be used to open a new account while the ban applies.

Nothing else is kept, it is not used for any other purpose, and it is deleted when the period ends.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (in the EU/EEA and UK under the GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access and portability – get a copy of your data: email us at cheshmak.support@gmail.com.
  • Rectification – correct your profile at any time in Edit Profile, or ask us to correct other data.
  • Erasure – delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account → Delete my account. If you cannot sign in, use our web page: https://sakhirezayi.github.io/cheshmak-legal/account-deletion.en.html.
  • Restriction of processing in certain cases.
  • Objection to processing based on legitimate interests, including safety processing; we will stop unless we have compelling grounds (for example, evidence of abuse).
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it (for example sensitive profile data: remove it from your profile).
  • Complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work, or to our lead authority: Belgian Data Protection Authority (Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit).

We answer requests within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests, in which case we tell you). We may need to confirm it is you, for example by asking you to sign in or reply from your account email. Requests are free unless clearly unfounded or excessive.

11. Security

Photos are stored in a private storage bucket and shown only through short-lived signed links, only to people allowed to see them. Database access is controlled per user by row-level security. Moderator tools are limited to named moderators. No system is perfect: if a breach is likely to put you at high risk, we will tell you without undue delay.
