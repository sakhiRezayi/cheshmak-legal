Delete your Cheshmak account

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · Published at https://sakhirezayi.github.io/cheshmak-legal/account-deletion.en.html

This page is for the Cheshmak dating app (Android, package app.cheshmak.dating) by {{CONTROLLER_NAME}}.

Option 1 – In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Cheshmak and sign in.
  2. Open Settings (Profile tab → Settings, or the “Open settings” button at the top of Discover).
  3. Scroll to Delete account and tap Delete my account.
  4. Tap Yes, delete.
  5. Enter your password to confirm it is you. If you signed in more than 5 minutes ago, the app asks for your password again.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out. This also works if your account has been suspended or banned: the restricted-account screen has a Delete my account button.

Option 2 – Without the app

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in:

  1. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email address of your Cheshmak account with the subject “Delete my account”.
  2. We send a confirmation link or code to that address. This proves the account is yours, so no one else can delete it.
  3. After you confirm, we delete the account within 7 days and at the latest within one month, and email you when it is done.

We never ask for your password by email.

What is deleted

  • Your email address, password and all sign-in sessions
  • Your profile (name, birth date, gender, bio, goal, interests, city, language, optional profile details and prompt answers, settings)
  • Your approximate location, if one was stored
  • All your photos
  • Your likes, passes, matches, read markers and blocks
  • Your messages (with the exception below)
  • Rate-limit counters

On your phone the app also forgets your age check, your onboarding drafts and its cached photos.

Delete only some data

You do not have to delete your account to remove data: you can delete photos, edit or clear your profile text, turn off discovery, or block people in the app. For anything else, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
