1. The service

Cheshmak is a dating app for Afghans in Afghanistan and abroad, available on Android. You can create a profile, browse other profiles, like, pass or super like, match and chat by text with your matches. Messaging after a match is always free.

2. Age: 18 or older

You must be 18 or older to use Cheshmak. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it.

3. Rules of conduct

  • Use your own, true details. Do not pretend to be someone else.
  • Do not harass, threaten, scam or pressure anyone.
  • Do not post nudity, sexual photos, hate speech, or anything illegal.
  • Do not use the app for advertising, spam or to find minors.

You can report and block any person in the app. We review reports. We may remove content, warn you, restrict or ban your account, and report serious cases to the authorities. You can appeal a decision by writing to the contact below.

4. Cheshmak Plus subscription

Plus is a paid subscription (weekly, monthly or every three months). Prices and billing periods are shown in Google Play before you buy.

  • The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it.
  • You cancel in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). We cannot cancel it for you inside the app.
  • After you cancel, Plus stays active until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • Refunds follow Google Play’s rules.

5. One-time purchases

  • Boost makes your profile more visible for 30 minutes.
  • Super likes are bought in packs. Each super like uses one from your balance.
  • If Google Play refunds a purchase, the matching balance is taken back.

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account. Deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play first. See our Privacy Policy for what is deleted and what we keep.
